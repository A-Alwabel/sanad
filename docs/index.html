Run AI models too big for any one device — together.

The best open models are free to download and impossible to run — 400 GB to 1 TB of weights no personal device can hold. Sanad chains ordinary devices into one: each holds a slice of a model's layers, and together they serve what none of them could alone.

The gap Sanad fills

Two ideas each worked once. Nobody had run both — until now.

Verified against the whole landscape as of August 2026. Sharding without incentives died. Incentives without sharding can't serve big models. Sanad is the join.

Sharding

Petals

✕ swarm dead since ~2024

Split a big model across volunteer devices — proven in 2022, it served BLOOM-176B and Llama-70B. But it paid contributors nothing, so the volunteers drifted away and the swarm went quiet.

Fair incentive

AI Horde

✓ alive and healthy

Non-tradeable credits: contribute compute, earn priority when you use the network — credits can never be bought or sold. It works, and it lasts. But every worker must host a whole model, so the big ones are out of reach.

Petals' sharding + Horde's fairness = Sanad

How it stays fair & alive

A network that breathes with its community.

01 · grows

Capacity ladder

The network serves the largest model the pooled memory can hold. More devices join, a bigger model comes online — automatically. Fewer devices, it steps down gracefully and keeps serving.

02 · respects you

The polite node

Your device, your priority — always. It runs below everything else, senses when you need the machine, drains out on its own, and rejoins when you're done. Leaving is never punished; your credits are kept.

03 · pays fairly

Weighted credits

Serve more layers, earn a bigger share of every token. Memory lent equals share earned. Credits buy queue priority and nothing else — non-tradeable by the rules, forever. No token, no speculation.

What Sanad is not.

Over-promising is how this field loses trust, so the honest limits get their own section — not a footnote. This is the brand, not the fine print.

not

A cheaper or faster API.

Centralized free tiers beat a swarm on speed every time. Sanad's value is resilience and ownership — not benchmarks. Selling it as "free inference" is part of what killed Petals.

not

A crypto project.

Credits are non-tradeable by the Terms of Service, forever. No token, no coin, no speculation, nobody's savings at risk. It's a founding, constitutional commitment.

not

Private yet.

Activations pass through the nodes that serve you, and prompts can be reconstructed from them — an unsolved research problem across the whole field. Sanad starts with trusted operators and treats privacy as open work, out loud.

not

Finished.

It's day one: one machine, trusted nodes, small models. Two houses on two networks is the next milestone. What's proven is proven; what isn't is labeled. That's the deal.

RFC 0001 · The Human Loop · in review

The next chapter is open for argument.

Rate answers, earn credits, and build the first fully open human-preference dataset — released with the methods used to collect it. The defenses come first (every reward-for-ratings system in history was gamed within days); training is deferred until the data earns it. The design is public, and its hardest questions are genuinely unanswered.

open question

Recognition alone, or small vested credits — which better resists gaming without crowding out goodwill?

open question

How large a poisoned fraction must a small community dataset survive before any model is trained on it?

open question

Should Sanad share policy language with Haidra, who carry three years of scar tissue from exactly this fight?
