# Governance

**Status:** founding document, adopted August 2026 by the project founder.
It is deliberately short. These are commitments, not aspirations — the project
asks to be held to them in public, from day one.

## Founding commitments

### 1. Credits are never for sale

Sanad credits exist for one purpose: fairness. You earn them by contributing
compute or work to the network; you spend them as priority when you consume.
They can **never** be bought, sold, traded, exchanged for money or
cryptocurrency, or made convertible into anything of monetary value — by us or
by anyone operating under this name. This follows the design proven by AI
Horde's kudos system, and it is what keeps Sanad free of speculation, token
economics, and securities questions.

This commitment is permanent. **Any fork, derivative, or governance change
that introduces the purchase or sale of credits forfeits all right to the
Sanad name and identity.** The Apache-2.0 license means anyone may fork the
code — that is by design — but a network where credits are for sale is not
Sanad and must not call itself Sanad.

### 2. The network stays open

The models Sanad serves are open-weight models, and access to the network's
inference remains openly available. There will be no paywalled tier of the
network itself, no gating of models behind payment, and no exclusive access
arrangements. Priority is allocated by contributed work (credits), never by
money. Outputs are the user's; the network claims no rights over them.

### 3. Nonprofit-intent stewardship

Sanad is public infrastructure, not a business. Until a formal structure
exists, the project operates as if nonprofit obligations already applied: no
monetization of the network, no sale of user data, no arrangement that gives
any party a financial claim on the network's operation.

The stated aspiration, once a real community exists: either establish an
independent foundation, or place Sanad under an existing public-AI nonprofit
umbrella — Haidra (the nonprofit behind AI Horde) and the Public AI Inference
Utility ecosystem are the obvious candidates, and we would rather join proven
stewards than duplicate them. The choice will be made in public, with the
community.

### 4. Decisions happen in public

All project decisions — technical, organizational, and financial (should there
ever be finances) — are made in public GitHub issues, discussions, and pull
requests. No private roadmap, no side agreements.

**Current maintainer:** [@A-Alwabel](https://github.com/A-Alwabel) (founder).
This is a bootstrap arrangement, not a goal. As soon as the project has
enough sustained contributors for a steering group to be meaningful, one will
be formed and this document updated to transfer authority to it. Until then,
the maintainer's decisions are subject to the commitments above and open to
challenge by anyone, in public.

## Amendments

Commitments 1–3 may be amended only to *strengthen* them; no process exists,
and none will be added, to weaken or remove them. Everything else in this
document may be changed by public proposal (an issue or RFC), open discussion,
and — once a steering group exists — its decision.

## Why so strict?

Because the projects Sanad builds on show both failure modes. Petals proved
the technology and died without an incentive layer. Countless
"decentralized AI" projects added tradeable tokens and became speculation
vehicles instead of infrastructure. AI Horde survived by holding one line:
contribution earns priority, and priority is never for sale. Sanad starts on
that line and stays there.
